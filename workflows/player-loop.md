# Player loop

[Portfolio index](../README.md)

| Stage | Player decision | System response | Failure explanation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Deploy | Which role and location justify the cost? | Preview eligibility and coverage; spend only on a valid order. | Insufficient resources, invalid sector or unmet support requirement. |
| Detect | Where is reconnaissance needed? | Contacts progress through detection/identification and become stale. | Hidden or stale targets remain ineligible for unsupported attacks. |
| Command | Move, defend, cross or attack? | Selected forces accept a route or eligible target. | Terrain, readiness, ammunition or target mismatch. |
| Resolve | Continue, reposition or withdraw? | Movement, engagement effects and state changes provide feedback. | Report the game reason an order failed. |
| Sustain | Repair, replenish or expand? | Resources and support restore capability over time. | Cost, delay or supply limits. |
| Complete | Hold objectives or attempt a new plan? | Explicit outcome and replay options. | Explain objective state, not real-world military advice. |

A player should complete this loop through visible controls before needing advanced grouping, layers or cinematic features.
