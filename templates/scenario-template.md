# Scenario: [name]

**Player goal:** One sentence.  
**Expected duration:** Design target, not a measured average.  
**Starting state:** Seed, sectors, units and resources.  
**Decision tension:** What must the player trade off?  
**Opposition:** Objectives, detection constraints and decision cadence.  
**Terrain:** Crossings, obstacles and eligible deployment areas.  
**Victory / defeat:** Explicit conditions for each.  
**Difficulty:** Which parameters change and why?  
**Feedback:** What the player sees after a valid or invalid order.  
**Verification:** Determinism, reachable outcomes and core-loop checks.  
**Balance evidence:** Not measured until playtested.  
**Boundary:** Fictional military sites and normalized capabilities.
