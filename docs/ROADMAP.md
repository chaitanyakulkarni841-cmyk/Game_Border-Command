# Outcomes and next priorities

[Portfolio index](../README.md)

## Delivered artifacts

I have a public playable game, seven recorded development stages, the full v0.7 source snapshot and this product portfolio. I use the documentation to connect what I wanted players to experience with the decisions, constraints and checks behind it.

The current update adds scoped coverage increases, passive upkeep, EMPTY indicators, attackable fictional infrastructure, a cumulative mission scoreboard and interpolated movement. Automatic local defence, incident navigation, command views and pinned Forces & Build remain. These are implementation outputs. They are not evidence of increased engagement or improved usability.

## Priorities

| Order | Next step | Why now | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Validate upkeep, coverage and mission-report clarity. | Confirm recovery choices remain meaningful and consequences are understood. | Observed first-session results with errors, time-to-response and limitations. |
| 2 | Observe five first-time players. | Test the central promise of intuitive play. | Task results, friction notes and limitations published. |
| 3 | Fix the most common core-loop failures. | Reduce confusion before adding content. | Repeat affected tasks with new participants. |
| 4 | Validate browser, GPU and touch behaviour. | Establish where the game is usable and responsive. | Device matrix and reproducible defects. |
| 5 | Tune travel time and scenario pacing. | A larger map can introduce waiting. | Scenario-specific observation and measured pacing. |
| 6 | Expand presentation and scenarios selectively. | Add variety after reliability and clarity are demonstrated. | Explicit hypothesis, cost and acceptance test for each addition. |

## Deferred choices

Multiplayer, cloud saves, a more advanced 3D battle view and a larger roster would increase implementation and support demands. I would take these on after validating the basic experience.

## Evidence discipline

I distinguish what I implemented, what I checked and what still needs evidence from players. Retention, adoption and enjoyment remain open questions. I would use the [experiment template](../templates/experiment-template.md) to record the next decision.
